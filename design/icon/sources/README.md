# Daptastic SVG masters

All artwork is editable vector geometry; no embedded raster images or external dependencies.

Selected design: concept B, the memory card with aligned left edges.

- `daptastic-icon-B.svg`: selected complete icon, 1024 × 1024.
- `daptastic-icon-A.svg`, `daptastic-icon-C.svg`: alternative concept masters, 1024 × 1024.
- `layer-0-background.svg`: full-square amber-to-orange background, 1024 × 1024.
- `layer-1-body.svg`: flat cream SD card and gold contacts on transparency, 1024 × 1024.
- `layer-2-star.svg`: flat gold star on transparency, 1024 × 1024.
- `menubar-idle.svg`: SD card with star, 18 × 18.
- `menubar-syncing.svg`: SD card with sync arrows, 18 × 18.
- `menubar-attention.svg`: SD card with star and isolated attention dot, 18 × 18.

The three layers share the same artboard and origin; preserve both when exporting. Menu-bar masters use black shapes and strokes on transparency. Their copies in `menubar/` and `Daptastic.xcassets/` are identical to these masters.
