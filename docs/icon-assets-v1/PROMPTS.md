# Image generation provenance

The built-in image generation tool produced the exploratory references in `references/`. Final production assets were authored as SVG geometry and exported to PNG to ensure clean edges, exact layer registration, flat fills, and deterministic sizes. The source of truth is the project's `docs/icon-brief.md`.

## Concept A

Design a macOS app icon: single bold simple object centered on a rounded-square squircle background. Flat shapes with soft subtle depth, generous margins, no text letters or logos, no fine outlines. Recognizable at 16px. Background warm vertical gradient #FFB347 top to #FF6A3D bottom. Cream body #FFF4E2 with darker cream #F2E2C6 controls, navy screen #1F2340, gold star #FFC93C and subtle #FFE08A top highlight. No recognizable branded hardware design, no Apple Music/iPod/Sony styling, no photorealism, busy details, thin lines or shadows outside squircle. Deliver one 1024x1024 PNG icon with transparent corners outside squircle, no presentation mockup. Concept A: chunky friendly portable music player straight-on tilted 8 degrees clockwise, occupying about 60% of canvas height. Rounded cream rectangle taller than wide, large navy screen in upper two thirds, a single solid darker-cream round control wheel below (no concentric ring, labels or markings). A very large five-point gold star fills screen, subtle glow. Broad proportions, simple charming toy-like geometry.

## Production decisions

Concept A is preferred by the brief. Its production body and star share the identical 8-degree rotation and 1024-square coordinate system across the flattened icon and flat layers. The production version simplifies bevels, removes raster glow, increases clear margins, and preserves a large star for tiny sizes. The app icons use a subtle contained shadow and a gold highlight gradient. Composer foreground layers contain only flat palette colors. The menu glyphs are hand-authored black SVG geometry on transparency.
